GeoNames geographical database
==============================

Polaris bundles a reduced extract of the GeoNames database
(https://www.geonames.org/): the cities5000 dump (towns of 5,000 people or
more), admin1CodesASCII and countryInfo, cut down to name, ASCII name,
first-level division, country, coordinates and population. It is embedded in
NINA.Polaris.dll as Resources/geo/cities5000.tsv.gz and countries.tsv and is
what the Observatory location search answers from when the host has no
internet.
