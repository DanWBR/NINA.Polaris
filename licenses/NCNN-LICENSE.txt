ncnn (libncnn.so)
=================

N.I.N.A. Polaris can optionally accelerate the GraXpert AI models
(background extraction, denoise v2) on any Vulkan GPU — e.g. the Adreno
643 of the Radxa Dragon Q6A via the Turnip driver, or Mali/Intel GPUs —
through Tencent's ncnn inference framework (Vulkan compute backend).

ncnn is an optional, separately-provided native runtime (libncnn.so,
staged via scripts/fetch-ncnn.sh). It is NOT committed to this repository
and is NOT part of the N.I.N.A. Polaris source. It is distributed by
Tencent under the BSD 3-Clause License, reproduced below.

The Vulkan loader (libvulkan.so.1) and the GPU driver (e.g. Mesa/Turnip)
come from the operating system and are licensed under their own terms
(typically MIT for the Vulkan-Loader and MIT for Mesa).
