SMBLibrary
==========

N.I.N.A. Polaris uses SMBLibrary, a pure-managed SMB1/SMB2 client, for the
SMB/CIFS backend of the "auto-push images to network storage" feature
(NINA.Polaris.Services.Storage.SmbStorageTarget). It writes captured
FITS/XISF files to a NAS, Windows share or Samba server.
