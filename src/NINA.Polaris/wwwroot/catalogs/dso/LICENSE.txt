Polaris Astro Controller bundled DSO catalog (dso.db)
==============================================

This SQLite database aggregates the following astronomical catalogs.
All sources are public-domain or openly licensed; Polaris itself
(MPL 2.0) does not own the underlying data. Run
`scripts/build-dso-catalog.py` to rebuild from the original sources.

Caldwell cross-references
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The Caldwell numbering (C1..C109, Patrick Moore 1995) is a hand-
picked assignment of existing NGC / IC entries to Caldwell numbers.
The mapping table is embedded in scripts/build-dso-catalog.py as
public-domain coordinates; the underlying objects come from
OpenNGC.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
Stars (catalog = HR, Star, WR; scripts/build-star-catalog.py)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit D., Warren
W.H. Jr., 1991), VizieR V/50. Public domain; obtained through the
VizieR service (see the CDS acknowledgement above).

IAU Catalog of Star Names, IAU Working Group on Star Names (WGSN),
https://www.iau.org/public/themes/naming_stars/ (file maintained at
https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~emamajek/WGSN/IAU-CSN.txt). Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The proper names
are attached to the Bright Star rows above; stars with no Bright
Star entry become rows of their own.

VIIth Catalogue of Galactic Wolf-Rayet Stars (van der Hucht K.A.,
2001, New Astronomy Reviews 45, 135), VizieR III/215. Obtained
through the VizieR service (see the CDS acknowledgement above).

A few common nicknames (Hind's Crimson Star, La Superba, Herschel's
Garnet Star, Merrill's Star) are added by the build script.
